01 · How a call works

From "hello" to a booked appointment in one conversation.

  1. 1

    Caller speaks

    Vapi answers the phone. It turns speech into text, runs the LLM with Ellie's system prompt, and speaks the reply back.

  2. 2

    Ellie picks a tool

    Once she has a name, a spelled-out email and a time, she calls GetSlots, BookSlot, UpdateSlots or CancelSlots.

  3. 3

    n8n does the work

    Each tool is a webhook. The workflow checks Google Calendar for conflicts, creates, moves or deletes the event, and writes the change to Airtable.

  4. 4

    Result comes back

    n8n replies with {"results":[{"toolCallId","result"}]}, and Ellie reads the result to the caller.

  5. ↺

    After hang-up

    Vapi posts an end-of-call-report to /callresults. It includes the transcript, recording, AI summary and cost, and all of it is upserted into Airtable's Call Recording table.

04 · The agent prompt

Personality up front, strict rules underneath.

Identity & style

"Hi, this is Ellie with Harrison Climate Solutions. How can I help you?"
  • Friendly and conversational, with a little Texan warmth and humor
  • Uses natural fillers like "Umm…" and "Well…", and keeps answers short
  • Steers off-topic questions back to HVAC services and scheduling
  • Reads times naturally ("Three thirty PM") and phone numbers digit by digit

Hard rules

  • Doesn't move forward until she has a correct name, email and time
  • Always says "Can you spell your email please?"
  • Defaults to a 30-minute appointment when no end time is given
  • Never books weekends, holidays or times in the past (with a joke about time travel)
  • Always calls GetSlots before booking and only offers times it returns, 2–3 at a time
  • Before cancelling, offers to reschedule instead
  • Transfers to Sam right away for emergencies or the secret phrase "Hot Brisket"

05 · Slot engine

How Ellie finds "I've got 1, 1:30 or 4."

If the requested time is busy, the Available Start Times & Ranges Code node takes the sorted week of events and, for each weekday:

  1. Starts at the beginning of the workday in America/Chicago
  2. Walks through the events and keeps every gap between them
  3. Splits each gap into 30-minute start times
  4. Marks 3 or more back-to-back slots as a wide open range
  5. Skips Saturdays and Sundays, then formats everything as text for the LLM

The timeline shows the engine running on the demo calendar right now, so it updates when you book something.
